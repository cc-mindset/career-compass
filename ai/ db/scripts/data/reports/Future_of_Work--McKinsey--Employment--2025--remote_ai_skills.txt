Future of Work Report 2025
McKinsey & Company

Executive Summary

The workplace landscape continues to evolve rapidly as organizations adapt to technological advancements and shifting employee expectations. This report examines key trends shaping employment in 2025.

Key Findings:

1. Remote Work Adoption
- 65% of knowledge workers now operate in hybrid or fully remote arrangements
- Companies report 23% increase in productivity for remote-eligible roles
- Geographic barriers to hiring continue to diminish

2. AI Integration
- 78% of organizations have implemented AI tools in at least one department
- Workers spending average of 4.5 hours per week using AI assistants
- New job categories emerging around AI management and prompt engineering

3. Skills Gap Challenges
- Critical shortage in data analysis and digital literacy skills
- 89% of employers investing in upskilling programs
- Median time to fill technical positions increased to 52 days

4. Compensation Trends
- Tech sector salaries increased 8.2% year-over-year
- Benefits packages expanding to include mental health and wellness support
- Flexible work arrangements now considered equivalent to 15% salary premium

Recommendations:

Organizations should prioritize investment in employee development, embrace flexible work models, and integrate AI thoughtfully to remain competitive in attracting and retaining talent.
